Description

Refurbished semi-detached family home on an elevated site in a cul de sac with lovely views at the top of Earlscourt, just off the Dunmore Road. Convenient access to University Hospital Waterford and all local amenities.

Accommodation

Tiled entrance hall, tiled Guest WC. Living room with laminate wood flooring and gas fireplace, double doors to dining room. Study / 4th bedroom with laminate wood flooring. Dining room with laminate wood flooring. Tiled kitchen / diner with oak units. Tiled utility with glazed door to side. On the first floor are an additional three bedrooms with laminate wood flooring, two of which have built in wardrobes. Lastly, there is a smartly upgraded shower room with a double shower. The garden to the rear is bordered by a lovely cut stone wall with a garden shed and patio. Lawn and drive to front.
